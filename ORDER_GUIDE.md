# Ordering the board

Two fab packages, both generated by `kicad/build_all.sh`:

| File | What | Size |
|---|---|---|
| `kicad/la4440-gerbers.zip` | one board, 2 layers, Gerbers + Excellon drill | 90 x 70 mm |
| `kicad/panel/la4440-panel-2up-gerbers.zip` | two boards joined by 4 mm tabs with mouse bites | 90 x 142 mm |

## Which one

- **Cheapest:** the single board. 90 x 70 mm fits the 100 x 100 mm prototype tier at PCBWay and JLCPCB
  (5 or 10 pieces). The 2-up panel is above that tier and only makes sense if the fab counts panels, not
  boards, in your quote.
- Options: 2 layers, 1.6 mm, 1 oz copper, any colour, HASL or ENIG. No stencil needed.

## Before you order

1. Open `kicad/la4440.kicad_pro` in KiCad 9 and look at the board yourself - nothing here has been built yet.
2. Check the electrolytic diameters against what you will fit: the footprints are 8 mm (47 uF) and
   12.5 mm (1000 / 2200 uF) cans at 3.5 / 5 mm lead pitch.
3. The LA4440 sits on the top edge with its tab outward. The heatsink hangs over the board edge;
   ground it with a wire from one tab screw to the J7 lug.
